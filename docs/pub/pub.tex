\hypertarget{introduction}{%
\section{Introduction}\label{introduction}}

Prompting in English yields improved performance across many
cross‐lingual tasks. For example, Jiang et al.~(2019) report increased
accuracy in knowledge extraction when using English prompts versus
diversified manual prompts. Lin et al.~(2021) observe that, for natural
language understanding and machine translation across 30 languages,
English prompts outperform multilingual configurations and even surpass
baselines such as GPT‐3. Similarly, Muennighoff et al.~(2023), Nie et
al.~(2024), Tu et al.~(2022), and Zhang et al.~(2023) report notable
gains measured by accuracy, F1 scores, and BLEU when models are prompted
in English.
